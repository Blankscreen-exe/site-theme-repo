ExpenTable themes

Eight directions. Each page shows the same four screens: Today, Edit Tasks, Categories and the Week table.

1. Midnight TealThe current look, refined: charcoal with a teal accent and rounded corners everywhere.
2. PaperLight and calm, like a notebook page: warm off-white, ink text, serif headings, one muted red accent.
3. Logo PopPlayful and built from the wordmark: cream background, one logo colour per category, chunky rounded cards.
4. NordCool and quiet for night use: slate blue-grey, a frost-blue accent, soft green for done.
5. SunsetA warm dark theme: deep plum with a coral-to-amber accent, large radius, a soft glow on the active day.
6. MonoMinimal black and white on pure black for OLED screens. The single accent appears only on today and on done tasks.
7. ForestNatural and soft: a sage background, cream cards, a moss-green accent and relaxed, rounded shapes.
8. TerminalRetro and a bit nerdy: green monospace text on near-black, amber for today, square corners and dashed borders.
