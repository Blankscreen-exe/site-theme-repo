Theme concepts

Ten directions, each applied to the two surfaces that decide it: the page a client opens and the list Maya works in. Same markup and the same waveform in every one, so what you are comparing is the design rather than the layout. Each carries a paste-ready token block.

Warm paper

One idea — warm off-white ground, warm ink — in five accents. They vary only in colour; all open light, all outline their surfaces.

Contrast on every pair that carries text was measured in a browser rather than judged by eye — the first pass had six failures, all in accent colours that pass on pure white and fail on warm paper.

Adopting one means editing three places: src/app/tokens.css, the hex mirror in opengraph-image.tsx (Satori resolves neither CSS variables nor oklch), and the hardcoded hex in lib/email.ts. The two “no outlines” concepts additionally need component changes, since borders are in the markup rather than in a token.
